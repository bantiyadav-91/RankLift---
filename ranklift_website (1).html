How RankLift works

🌐

Free website advertising

Submit your website, add a short description, and get a place in the public directory. Submissions are reviewed before they appear.

📣

Optional sponsored visibility

Support the platform with a donation or choose a clearly labeled promotional spot. Sponsorship changes placement on RankLift only, not search engine rankings.

🧭

Useful discovery

Visitors can search by topic and find interesting sites without confusing paid promotion with community picks.

🛡️

Transparent ranking

Directory picks can use relevance, quality, and community feedback. Payment is never presented as an organic Google ranking boost.

Keep the directory growing

Support independent websites

Donations help cover hosting and moderation. You can support the project without buying a ranking. This demo does not process payments.
